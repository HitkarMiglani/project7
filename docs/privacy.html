KnapResume · Privacy Policy

Your career data
stays yours

This policy covers the KnapResume local web app (python app.py), the static browser demo, and the CLI (python main.py).

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Short version: KnapResume is local-first. Your profile, facts, and generated PDFs live in a SQLite file and an outputs/ folder on your own machine. Content leaves your machine only when you trigger generation — sent to the AI provider and company-research service you configured.

Overview

KnapResume ("we," "our," or "us") runs on your computer. There is no KnapResume cloud, no user accounts, and no analytics pipeline phoning home. This policy explains what is stored where, and what leaves your device when you use each feature.

Deleting knapresume.db and outputs/ removes all app-held data. Uninstalling means deleting the project folder — nothing remains on any server, because there isn't one.

What leaves your machine (only when you ask)

AI providers — Claude / Gemini

When you click Tailor PDFs (or run main.py tailor), your knapsack-selected resume facts and the target job description are sent to the provider you chose — Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini) — using the API key from your .env or the key field. Their handling of that content is governed by their own policies:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy (Gemini API)

Only the allocated subset of facts is sent — not your entire database. Review your provider's data-handling terms before sending sensitive material.

Company research — Brave Search (optional)

Unless you pass --skip-web, tailoring fetches public company context via the Brave Search API using your BRAVE_API_KEY. Only job-description text and the posting URL are used as the query — your resume is never sent there.

Browser demo build

The static demo page sends content directly from your browser to the provider you select. There is no KnapResume backend in that build at all.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics beacons.
  • No sale of personal information — there is nothing to sell.
  • No third-party workspaces or ad networks. (Earlier experimental integrations were removed; run history lives only in your local run_logs table.)
  • No mobile apps, no ads, no advertising identifiers.

Permissions

  • Internet access: used to reach your AI provider and (optionally) Brave Search. Everything else works offline once models are cached.
  • File access: used so you can pick resume / job-description files and export PDFs on your device.

Data retention

  • Local app: data persists until you delete it (profiles can be deleted in the UI; the DB file can be removed outright).
  • Static demo: nothing is stored by any project-operated server — content exists in your browser session only.
  • Anything sent to Anthropic, Google, or Brave is retained under their policies and your settings with them.

Data security

Local-first is itself the main safeguard: your data never traverses infrastructure we operate. API keys live in your local .env (git-ignored), are injected server-side at page render, and are explicitly excluded from logs. Still, no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure — treat API keys like passwords and rotate any key you suspect is exposed.

Children

KnapResume is not directed to children under 13. It processes whatever resume content the user provides locally; if you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the product changes (e.g. the LaTeX template engine, ATS evaluator, or export formats on the v2 roadmap). The effective date at the top shows the current version.
